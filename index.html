5. Working Principle

  • RC Low-Pass: The capacitor shunts high-frequency signals; output is taken across the capacitor.
  • RC High-Pass: The capacitor blocks DC and attenuates low frequencies; output is across the resistor.
  • RL Low-Pass: The inductor impedance increases with frequency; output is across the resistor.
  • RL High-Pass: The inductor impedance increases with frequency; output is across the inductor.
